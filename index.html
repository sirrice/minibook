One-page mini-zine creator

Transform your PDF into an 8-page booklet from one sheet, printed on one side. Print, fold, cut – done!

📖 How to fold your zine

▼
  1. Print the single-page PDF on one side of a sheet
  2. Fold the paper in half lengthwise (hotdog fold)
  3. Fold in half again, then once more (you'll have 8 sections)
  4. Unfold once so you see 4 sections
  5. Cut along the center fold between the two middle sections
  6. Unfold completely, fold lengthwise, and push the ends together - it will twist into a booklet!

How to use this tool

▼

📄 What does this do?

This tool takes your PDF (up to 8 pages) and rearranges it into a special layout that, when printed on one side and folded, becomes an 8-page booklet from a single sheet of paper. Perfect for zines, mini-comics, poetry chapbooks, or any small publication!

📤 Step 1: Upload your PDF

Select a PDF file with 1-8 pages. Each page will become one panel in your mini-zine. If you have fewer than 8 pages, the remaining pages will be blank.

⚙️ Step 2: Configure margin

Margin – Controls spacing and sizing:

  • Positive values (e.g., 18) – Adds white space around the edges. Use for safety margins
  • Negative values (e.g., -18) – Makes content bigger by cropping edges. Good for full-bleed or text-heavy pages
  • Zero (0) – No margin, content fills each panel exactly

🖨️ Step 3: Print

After processing, you'll see a single-page PDF. Print settings:

  • Print in LANDSCAPE orientation
  • Use 100% scale or "Actual Size" (NOT "Fit to Page")
  • Print single-sided (one side only)
  • Use standard US Letter size paper (8.5" × 11")

✂️ Step 4: Fold and cut

Follow the diagram above to fold and cut your printed sheet into a booklet. The magic happens when you push the folded ends together!
